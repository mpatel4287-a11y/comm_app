Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026 | Version 1.0.0

1. Introduction

Ramanagara Patidar Samaj ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting the privacy and personal information of our community members. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect through our mobile application and web portal, how it is utilized, how it is kept secure, and your individual privacy rights.

2. Information We Collect

To provide digital directory access, family tree navigation, event management, and firm networking, we collect the following data:

  • Personal Identifiers: Full name, Member Identification Number (MID), family name, date of birth, age, gender, and blood group.
  • Contact Information: Mobile phone numbers, residential addresses, and email addresses.
  • Family & Community Connections: Family lineage (Head of Family, relationships, sub-families), business/firm profiles, and committee designations.
  • Media & Photos: Profile photos, business logos, and event photos uploaded voluntarily by members or administrators.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens to deliver community announcements and meeting alerts.
  • Biometric Data: If enabled, fingerprint or facial recognition credentials are processed exclusively on your local device hardware by the operating system and are never sent to or stored on our servers.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively for internal community welfare and connectivity:

  • Maintaining a secure and searchable directory for verified community members.
  • Visualizing interactive family tree structures.
  • Sending essential community announcements, event invitations, and circulars.
  • Promoting community-owned businesses and entrepreneurship.
  • Generating digital member identity cards for verification at community gatherings.

🔒 Strict No-Sale & No-Spam Policy

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to commercial advertisers or third-party marketing brokers. Data access is restricted to verified community members and authorized administrators.

4. Data Storage & Security

All application data is securely hosted on Google Cloud Firebase (Firestore and Cloud Storage). All network traffic is encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Multi-factor administrative controls ensure that sensitive record modifications are restricted to authorized personnel.

5. Account & Data Deletion

In compliance with Google Play Store data safety guidelines and global privacy standards, members have the right to request deletion of their account and all associated personal records at any time.

🗑️ How to Request Account Deletion

You can request account deletion through either of the following methods:

1. Inside the Mobile App: Go to Settings → Delete Account & Data → Confirm your request. Your account will be immediately deactivated and scheduled for permanent deletion.

2. Via Email: Send an email to support@ramanagarapatidar.org with your registered Full Name, Mobile Number, and MID with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Upon confirmation, all personal profiles, credentials, and uploaded images will be permanently erased from our active database within 14 business days.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for community households. Minor children’s information is recorded solely as dependents under their parent or legal guardian's family profile for accurate family lineage records. We do not independently market to or solicit data from children under 13.
